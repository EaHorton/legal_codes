
CHAPTER CXXXIX
An Act to Divorce John Chambers, of the County of Haywood, from His Wife Rainey.

Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of North Carolina, and it is hereby enacted by the authority of the same, that John Chambers, of the county of Haywood, be, and he is hereby divorced from the bonds of matrimony from his wife Rainey, in as full and ample a manner, as if the same had never been entered into; and that he be and is hereby discharged from all duties, obligations and engagements as husband to said Rainey.
