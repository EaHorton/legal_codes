DIVORCE AND ALIMONY.

An Act to amend the ninth section of the thirty-ninth chapter of the Revised Statutes, entitled “An Act concerning divorce and alimony.” 110.

Alimony may be decreed to a married woman for the support and maintenance of herself and family, pending a suit instituted by her for a divorce, 110.

The court may at any time during the pendency of the suit for cause shown, alter or modify said decree, 110.
