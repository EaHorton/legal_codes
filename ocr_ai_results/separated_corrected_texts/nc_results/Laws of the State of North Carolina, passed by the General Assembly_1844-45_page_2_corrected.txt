

Chapter XLII

An Act for the relief of Samuel Sowers.

Sec. I. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of North Carolina, and it is hereby enacted by the authority of the same, That Samuel Sowers of the county of Davidson, be, and he is hereby relieved and exonerated from all pains and penalties of the 14th section of the 39th chapter of the Revised Statutes, entitled an act concerning Divorce and Alimony.

Sec. II. That this act be in force from and after its ratification.

[Ratified this 7th day of January, 1845.]
